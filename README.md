# 🎤 voice-teleprompter-android - Your Voice, Your Teleprompter

## 🌟 What Is This?

**Suflyor (Суфлёр)** is a completely free and open-source teleprompter app for Android that **listens to you and scrolls automatically as you speak**. No buttons, no remotes, no tap-to-scroll — just talk naturally and the text follows your pace.

It’s perfect for:

- Recording **Instagram Reels** or **TikTok** videos
- Filming **YouTube** content
- Practicing speeches or presentations
- Recording video messages directly through your camera

Here’s the magic: the app recognizes **English and Russian speech entirely on your phone**. That means **no internet connection, no data sending, and zero privacy concerns**. It works fully offline.

## 🧭 How to Use Suflyor (Step-by-Step)

### 1️⃣ Prepare Your Script

- Open the app.  
- Tap the **"+"** button or **New Script** to create a new text.  
- Paste or type your speech.  
- You can edit font size, text color, and background color anytime.

### 2️⃣ Start the Float Mode

- Tap the **"Start"** button.  
- The app creates a **small floating bubble** (like a chat head) that appears above other apps.  
- You can drag this bubble anywhere on your screen.

### 3️⃣ Open Instagram, TikTok, or Your Camera

- Open TikTok or Instagram and start recording a video.  
- The floating teleprompter will appear on top of your camera view — just like a real studio prompter.

### 4️⃣ Speak Naturally

- Begin speaking your script.  
- The text **scrolls automatically** as it hears your words.  
- If you pause, the scrolling stops. If you speak faster, it scrolls faster.

### 5️⃣ Adjust on the Fly (Optional)

- While recording, you can drag the bubble to reposition it.  
- Tap the bubble to open quick controls: pause, resume, or change scroll speed temporarily.

## 🎯 Pro Tips for Best Results

- **Speak clearly** and at a steady pace. The app is very accurate, but your phone’s microphone needs to hear you well.
- **Use a quiet room** for best recognition.
- **Keep your phone at a comfortable distance** — about 20–30 cm from your mouth works great.
- **Test with a short script first** to get comfortable with the speed and flow.

## 🛠️ What If Something Goes Wrong?

- **If the app won’t install:** Make sure you allowed "Unknown Sources" (see Step 4). Also check that you downloaded the correct file.
- **If it doesn’t recognize your voice:** Try speaking a bit slower and closer to the mic. Also check if a popup asks for microphone permission — tap **Allow**.
- **If the floating bubble won’t show:** Make sure you granted "Display over other apps" permission when asked.
